 2.  INSTRUMENT FAILURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  NO FAILURES IN THE GRIDDED PRODUCT. All 39 rows return 100% depth and 100%
  temperature from 2016-06-05 to 2017-04-13. F3 is the cleanest record in the
  array and the natural control for F1 and F4.

  Three thermistors never made it into the product because they did not
  record at all -- 72212 (138 m), 72073 (278 m) and 72197 (1589 m) -- and one
  ADCP, 2625 at 1559 m, likewise. Those are pre-processing losses, not mid-
  record failures: nothing drops out partway through.

  The declining deep velocity coverage (98.8% at 1603 m down to 10.2% at 1633
  m) is ADCP range and side-lobe loss at the last few bins above the anchor,
  present from the first day. It is not a failure and it is not a reason to
  shorten the record; it is a reason not to trust the bottom four velocity
  levels.

  TWO ROWS ARE THE SAME INSTRUMENT. Rows 1 and 2 both carry SN 72206 at an
  identical mean depth of 62.5 m and identical coverage. One of them is a
  duplicate introduced in gridding. Fitting modes to both double-weights that
  level.

 6.  NOTES AND OPEN QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  MEAN OBSERVED DEPTHS RUN 6 to 15 m SHALLOWER THAN NOMINAL, growing with
  depth: -5.5 m at the top sensor, -15.8 m at the deepest. That is a wire
  length or lay-back correction, not noise, and it is monotonic.

  KNOCKDOWN IS THE SECOND LARGEST IN THE ARRAY: per-sensor depth range median
  124.6 m, max 149.6 m, against 56 m at F2.

  THE AS-DEPLOYED WIRE TABLE IS MISSING. The archived original of this file
  carries a wire table headed "F2" whose serial numbers are F2's throughout
  -- it is a copy-paste of the F2 table, not F3's. It has been left out here
  rather than reproduced wrongly. F3's own wire table has to come off the
  deployment sheet.

  F3 IS THE CONTROL. It is the only mooring with a full-length, failure-free
  record at full coverage, so it is the one to check pipeline behaviour
  against before trusting F1 or F4.
