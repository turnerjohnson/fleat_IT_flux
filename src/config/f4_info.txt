 2.  INSTRUMENT FAILURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  TWO FAILURES, AND THEY COST MORE THAN HALF THE RECORD.

  1. THE DEEP ADCP QUITS ON 2016-11-17. Sensors 25-33 (observed 636 to 823 m,
  the bottom two thirds of the column) return u and v with full coverage up
  to 2016-11-17 and almost nothing after it -- 53% overall. Sensor 24 (537 m)
  is the transition: 53% coverage with a last good sample on 2017-03-29, i.e.
  intermittent rather than dead.

  Nothing in the pipeline complains. The velocity fit extrapolates the bottom
  two thirds of the column from the top 450 m and returns plausible-looking
  amplitudes. This is the single most dangerous silent failure in the array,
  and it is why every other config in this directory carries an instruction
  to check coverage before anything else.

  cfg.t_end = 736650 (2016-11-16) stops two days short of it rather than
  trying to ride it out. That leaves 164 of 313 days.

  2. SENSOR 2 (Chi-POD 1116, 62 m) LOSES DEPTH AND TEMPERATURE ON 2017-02-06,
  at 78.8% coverage. This is after the cut and so costs the flux nothing, but
  it matters to any full-length T analysis.

  Sensor 34 (72140, 848 m) carries neither S nor velocity: it sits below the
  deepest ADCP bin, on the releases.

  CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ARRAY COMPARISON: F4's flux record is 164 days against
  F2's and F3's 313. Any array mean, and any statement about seasonality, has
  to say so. align_flux_records pads F4 with NaN rather than shortening
  everyone to it, and the movie has to tolerate F4 going blank partway
  through.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 6.  NOTES AND OPEN QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  KNOCKDOWN IS THE LARGEST IN THE ARRAY: per-sensor depth range median 164.6
  m and maximum 195.6 m, in 880 m of water. That is 22% of the water column.
  The array also settles about 25 m deeper around the same time the ADCP
  fails.

  MEAN OBSERVED DEPTHS RUN 10 to 19 m SHALLOWER THAN NOMINAL, growing with
  depth. The nominal depths in section 4 carry a .6 m offset from the
  deployment sheet; the observed column in section 3 is the one to plot
  against.

  SALINITY SPIKES ARE WHY THE T/S GATES EXIST. F4's gridded product carries
  salinity from -114 to +489 psu at the top five sensors, and a negative S
  makes sw_pden return a COMPLEX sigma that travels silently all the way into
  the interp1 inside f2_eta. Sensors below about 110 m never leave [34.38
  35.07], so the [33 36] window only bites at the top and costs 257 of 1.46
  million samples (0.018%).
