 6.  NOTES AND OPEN QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  UNRESOLVED -- 22 m DEPTH OFFSET AGAINST THE SPRAY GLIDERS. The mooring's
  thermocline sits about 22 m shallower than the gliders' over 85-250 m, and
  the offset collapses to zero below 300 m. At fixed depth the mooring reads
  colder. Ruled out: wire extrapolation (SBE37/SBE39 sensors with their own
  pressure show the same offset as the interpolated RBRs), time sampling, a
  wake gradient, and a water-mass difference. Open question for whoever knows
  the Spray format. CONSEQUENCE: do not splice glider sigma(z) onto the
  mooring. The glider T-S RELATION is fine, being depth-independent.

  MEAN OBSERVED DEPTHS RUN ~11 m SHALLOWER THAN NOMINAL over the top fifteen
  sensors and ~2 m deeper below 300 m. The nominal list in section 4 and the
  observed column in section 3 are not interchangeable in the thermocline.

  KNOCKDOWN: per-sensor depth range min 4.1 m, median 56.0 m, max 62.9 m.
  Episodic (Jul-Aug, Dec-Jan), not continuous.

  SALINITY IS NOT MEASURED AT 43 LEVELS. The readme says only "S:
  interpolated from glider data to 10 minute intervals", and S has the same
  8985 unique values at every depth from 107 to 1190 m, so it came from one
  procedure, not from 43 conductivity cells -- there are four. Whatever that
  procedure was, it stopped at 107 m.
