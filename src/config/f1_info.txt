 2.  INSTRUMENT FAILURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ONE FAILURE, AND IT ENDS THE USABLE RECORD AT 188 OF 313 DAYS.

  Every velocity and salinity channel on the mooring stops on 2016-12-10, and
  the gridded pressure (depth) record stops with them. Temperature does not:
  all 29 thermistors return 99.7% coverage through to 2017-04-13. So F1 has a
  full-length T record sitting on top of a half-length u, v, S and z record,
  and only the shorter one can carry a flux.

  cfg.t_end = 736675 (2016-12-11) is set at that boundary. Running past it
  does not fail loudly -- the fit simply has no velocity to fit -- which is
  the same trap F4 sets.

  SECOND, SMALLER FAILURE: sensor 5 (RBR 100327, nominal 164 m) loses both
  depth and temperature on 2016-10-16, at 13.9% coverage. It is the only
  thermistor on this mooring that dies early, and it is also the one whose
  mean observed depth (160.4 m) sits ABOVE sensor 4's (161.0 m), i.e. the two
  cross. Treat 100327 as suspect for the whole record, not just after
  October.

 6.  NOTES AND OPEN QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  BOTH McLANE PROFILERS RETURNED NO DATA. MM01 and MM02 are commented out of
  the deployment list, and that is why: they recorded nothing. They were the
  only instruments that would have sampled the 1380-3360 m gap, so their loss
  is what turns that gap from a sparse region into an empty one. Nothing else
  on the mooring can fill it -- the three deep instruments that might have
  helped also failed (ADCP 14255 at 1491 m, ADCP 22525 at 2304 m, SBE39 3254
  at 2304 m), and the two that did record below 1380 m sit on the anchor at
  3362 and 3374 m with temperature only.

  F1 IS INSTRUMENTED TO 1380 m IN 3390 m OF WATER. The deepest wire
  thermistor is 72162 at a nominal 1378 m, and the only two instruments below
  it (72168, 72169) sit on the anchor at 3362 and 3374 m with temperature
  only. So 59% of the water column, by depth, has no measurement between 1380
  m and the bottom. Flat-bottom modes computed on H = 3390 m are being fitted
  to data that spans the top 41% of that depth. This is the largest
  unresolved assumption at F1 and it is not shared by any other mooring in
  the array.

  KNOCKDOWN IS LARGE: the per-sensor depth range has a median of 116 m and a
  maximum of 205 m, against 56 m at F2. Isopycnal-following eta is immune to
  this by construction, but anything that assumes a fixed sensor depth is
  not.

  MEAN OBSERVED DEPTHS RUN 2 TO 4 m DEEPER than nominal on the wire, which is
  the opposite sign to F2, F3 and F4. Worth one look before trusting either
  number.
