 2.  INSTRUMENT FAILURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  NO FAILURES. All eight rows return 93.8% coverage, and the missing 6% is
  the pre-deployment lead-in in the common time grid, not a dropout: every
  channel starts on 2016-06-23 and runs to 2017-04-12 without a gap. The
  deployment is 18 days shorter at the front than the rest of the array
  because F6 went in last, so its record is 293 days, not 313.

  The one hole is sensor 8 (RBR 72141, 419 m): no salinity and no velocity.
  It hangs on the releases below the deepest ADCP bin.

  THE REAL PROBLEM AT F6 IS NOT FAILURE, IT IS RESOLUTION. The mooring
  carried 19 SBE 56 thermistors between 131 and 405 m, and NONE of them are
  in the gridded product -- it has 8 rows where the wire had 31 instruments.
  Over 434 m of water, 8 levels is a thin basis for six vertical modes.
  Either the SBE 56 records exist and have not been gridded, or they were
  lost; the archived original of this file lists them all, so the question is
  answerable.

  on the wire was used to BUILD that product -- their pressure fixes the
  depth interpolation, their conductivity is the source of S, the ADCPs are
  the source of u and v -- but none of them is a separate level you can fit
  to. So the 8 levels in section 3 are the vertical resolution available to
  the mode fit, not the 35 instruments in this list.

 6.  NOTES AND OPEN QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  F6 SITS ON A SLOPE THE BATHYMETRY GRID CANNOT RESOLVE. Within two
  kilometres the grid runs from 15 m to 1578 m at about 500 m spacing; the
  nearest cell reads 185 m and the 434 m isobath falls between cells. The 434
  m in this file is off the deployment sheet and is the number to use. Do not
  "correct" it against the grid -- fleat_moorings tests over a patch rather
  than a point precisely because of this mooring.

  The same steepness is why F6 is interesting and why it is hard: 434 m of
  water on a reef flank is a plausible generation site, and flat-bottom
  vertical modes are at their least defensible there.

  KNOCKDOWN IS THE SMALLEST IN THE ARRAY: per-sensor depth range median 32.7
  m, max 39.5 m. Mean observed depths run 2 to 10 m shallower than nominal.
